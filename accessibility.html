Accessibility Statement

Our patients are adults of every age, and many are older adults. A website you cannot read is a website that does not exist — so accessibility here is a clinical concern, not a checkbox.

1. Our commitment

Concept Health Clinic aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA across this website, and to exceed it wherever the audience calls for it. We treat accessibility as an ongoing practice: we test, we fix, and we welcome being told when something does not work.

2. What we've built in

  • Semantic structure — one clear main heading per page, ordered headings beneath it, landmark regions, and a logical reading order.
  • Skip link — the first thing you can tab to jumps straight to the main content.
  • Visible focus — every interactive element shows a strong focus ring; nothing relies on hover alone.
  • Descriptive alt text — every meaningful image is described; purely decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Labelled controls — form fields have real labels, not placeholder-only text, and errors are announced.
  • No colour-only meaning — status is always paired with text or an icon, never colour alone.
  • Touch targets — buttons and links meet a comfortable minimum size for less steady hands.

3. Readability for older adults

Because a large share of our patients are older adults, we tuned the site for them:

  • Body text at a comfortable size with generous line spacing.
  • High-contrast text against its background throughout.
  • Plain-language copy — medical terms are explained where they appear.
  • Large, clearly labelled buttons, with the phone number always visible.
  • The page remains fully usable when text is enlarged to 200%.

4. Keyboard and screen reader

Every feature is operable without a mouse: navigation, the testimonial slider, the FAQ accordion, the photo gallery and the chat assistant. The chat log announces new messages to screen readers, and Escape closes any open panel.

5. Motion and animation

This site uses animation to make content readable rather than busy. If your device or browser is set to reduce motion, the site automatically disables scroll animations, parallax effects, the marquee and smooth scrolling. The pop-up activity notices are also suppressed. Nothing is hidden when motion is off.

6. Known limitations

We would rather tell you than have you discover it:

  • The embedded Google Map is a third-party component we do not control, and its internal accessibility varies. Every map location also has a plain-text address and a "Get directions" link that opens Google Maps directly.
  • Environmental photographs are licensed stock imagery pending replacement with approved clinic photography; alt text describes what each image is intended to show.
  • The external patient portal is operated by eClinicalWorks and is outside the scope of this statement.

7. Accessibility in the clinic

This statement covers the website. If you need an accommodation for an in-person visit — a wheelchair-accessible route, extra time, a large-print document, a caregiver present, or an interpreter — call us before your appointment and we will arrange it. We would much rather plan ahead than improvise.

8. Tell us about a barrier

If any part of this site stops you from getting what you need, tell us and we will fix it — and we will help you directly in the meantime. You do not need to use a form.
