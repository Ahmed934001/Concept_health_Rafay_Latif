Meet your physician

Rafay Latif, MD, MHA

Board-certified in Internal Medicine and Geriatric Medicine, with 25+ years of hospital-based clinical experience.

Rafay Latif, MD, MHA · Internal Medicine & Geriatric Medicine · Denison, Texas

A physician, a healthcare leader and an educator

Rafay Latif is a board-certified Internal Medicine and Geriatric Medicine physician with 25+ years of experience in hospital-based medicine. His professional background includes clinical practice in academic medical centers, Veterans Affairs healthcare settings, and community hospitals, as well as leadership roles in hospital medicine and medical education.

With postgraduate training in Internal Medicine and Geriatric Medicine and a Master of Health Administration from Columbia University, Rafay Latif brings a comprehensive perspective to patient care — one that treats the clinical decision and the system around it as connected problems.

Credentials

  • Board-Certified: Internal MedicineComprehensive adult primary care across the full range of medical conditions.
  • Board-Certified: Geriatric MedicineSpecialized care for older adults, including complex multi-condition management.
  • Geriatric Medicine FellowshipNorthwell Health / Zucker School of Medicine at Hofstra University — Manhasset, New York.
  • Internal Medicine ResidencyColumbia University – Harlem Hospital Center — New York, New York.
  • Master of Health AdministrationColumbia University.
  • Former Chief HospitalistHarlem Hospital Center — hospital medicine leadership and clinical care.
  • Editor-in-Chief, StatPearls GeriatricsEditorial leadership in geriatric medical education.

What this means for your visit

Hospital medicine is where complexity concentrates — patients arrive with several active problems, unfamiliar medication lists, and families trying to hold a plan together. That experience shapes how this practice works: nothing is treated in isolation, and the medication list gets read carefully.

To schedule with Rafay Latif, MD, MHA, call (903) 600-2100. New patient visits are longer by design — expect a thorough history and a medication review at the first appointment.
