Who we are

About the Clinic

A practice built around one idea: adults and older adults deserve care that accounts for the whole person, not just the presenting complaint.

Rafay Latif, MD, MHA — Internal Medicine & Geriatric Medicine

Care that starts with understanding

Concept Health Clinic is an Internal Medicine and Geriatric Medicine practice in Denison, Texas. We see adults at every stage — from working-age patients managing a chronic condition to older adults navigating several conditions, several medications and several specialists at once.

Every new patient begins with a thoughtful, unhurried first visit. We take time to understand your medical history, review your medications, discuss your health goals, and create a clear, personalized care plan. No unnecessary pressure — just compassionate guidance and a better understanding of your health.

Our approach goes beyond addressing immediate symptoms. We consider your overall health, daily functioning, long-term needs, and the factors that matter most to your quality of life.

How we work

  • Time is the treatment. Rushed appointments miss things. We build the schedule around the conversation.
  • The whole picture. Function, mobility, nutrition and mood are reviewed alongside lab values and diagnoses.
  • Plain language. You should leave understanding your plan, not just possessing a printout.
  • Families welcome. With your consent, we involve the people who help you day to day.

What guides us

Three commitments shape every appointment, every phone call, and every decision we help you make.

Our mission

To provide compassionate, evidence-based Internal Medicine and Geriatric Care that supports health, independence, and quality of life through every stage of adulthood.

Our vision

To be North Texas' most trusted destination for personalized adult and senior healthcare.

Our values

  • Compassion

    We start with empathy, dignity and respect — every visit, every conversation.

  • Integrity

    Honest communication and transparent decisions, even when the news isn't simple.

  • Excellence

    Evidence-based medicine, sound clinical judgment and continuous learning.

  • Partnership

    We work with you, your family and your other clinicians as one team.

  • Respect

    Your priorities, preferences and pace set the agenda — never a template.

  • Prevention

    We look ahead: screenings, lifestyle and early action before problems grow.

  • Independence

    Function and mobility are medical outcomes. We protect them deliberately.

  • Innovation

    Quality improvement and modern care models, applied where they genuinely help.

  • Patient-First Care

    Every decision begins with what is best for the person in the room.
