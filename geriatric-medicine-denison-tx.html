Specialized geriatric medicine

Geriatric Medicine in Denison, TX

Specialized care for older adults: assessment that accounts for function and independence, medication review, falls risk, and goals of care.

A Geriatric Doctor in Denison, TX

As healthcare needs evolve with age, medical care often requires a more comprehensive perspective. Geriatric Medicine addresses the complex interaction of medical conditions, medications, mobility, functional independence, and overall well-being — because for an older adult, those are not separate problems.

A classic example: a patient is seen for dizziness. The medical explanation might be blood pressure. The functional explanation might be a medication that was appropriate at 55 and is now causing orthostatic drops at 82. The practical explanation might be loose rugs at home. Geriatric assessment looks for all three.

What geriatric care covers

  • Comprehensive geriatric assessment — medical, functional and cognitive factors reviewed together rather than one at a time.
  • Frailty prevention and falls risk evaluation — strength, balance, nutrition and medication-related risk assessed together, with practical prevention strategies.
  • Complex chronic conditions and polypharmacy review — when several conditions and many medications interact, someone needs to look at the whole list.
  • Transitions of care, goals of care and advance planning — continuity between hospital, home and rehabilitation, with medication information that matches reality.

The five things we ask about

Geriatric assessment is sometimes described as the "5 Ms". It is a useful checklist for what a standard visit can miss:

  • Mind — memory, mood and cognition, including changes the patient may not notice themselves.
  • Mobility — walking, balance and falls, because a fall changes everything downstream.
  • Medications — every prescription, supplement and over-the-counter item, reviewed for fit.
  • Multicomplexity — how multiple conditions interact, rather than treating each in isolation.
  • Matters most — what the person actually wants from their care, which is the foundation of every other decision.

Senior medical care in Denison TX — for families too

Geriatric care rarely involves only the patient. Adult children often manage appointments, medications and transport from a distance. With the patient's consent, we involve family and caregivers in the conversation, and we are explicit about what the plan is and who is doing what.

You do not need multiple diagnoses to see a geriatric physician. Many patients come with a single concern — a fall, a memory question, or a medication list that has grown without review. That is a reasonable place to start.
