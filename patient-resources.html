Preparing for your appointment

  • A government-issued photo ID
  • Your insurance card, if applicable
  • A current list of medications and supplements
  • Relevant medical records or recent test results
  • Information about recent hospitalizations
  • A list of symptoms, concerns, or questions
  • Contact details of other clinicians involved in your care

For geriatric appointments, information about recent falls, mobility changes, daily functioning, or medication difficulties may also be helpful.

Questions patients ask most

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

Call the clinic to confirm what your insurance plan requires. Many plans do not need a referral for a primary care or geriatric visit; some do. It is a two-minute phone call and it avoids a wasted trip.

Does the clinic accept my insurance?

Coverage varies by plan and changes between plan years. Call (903) 600-2100 with your plan details before your visit and we will tell you where things stand rather than leave you to find out at the front desk.

Can I bring a family member or caregiver?

Yes, absolutely! You are welcome to bring a family member or caregiver to your appointment for support, comfort, and assistance in discussing your healthcare needs.

What is the difference between Internal Medicine and Geriatric Medicine?

Internal Medicine covers adult health broadly. Geriatric Medicine adds a focus on older adults: how multiple conditions interact, how medications affect function, falls and mobility, and what matters most to the person. Both are provided at this clinic, so you do not have to choose before calling.

How do I request a prescription refill?

Use the patient portal or call the clinic during office hours. Refills are handled during normal business hours, so please allow time before you run out — and have the pharmacy details ready.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

Call 911. This website, the contact form and ordinary email are not emergency communication channels and are not monitored around the clock.

Please do not send medical information through this website. The contact form and ordinary email are not secure channels for protected health information. For anything clinical, call the clinic or use the patient portal.
