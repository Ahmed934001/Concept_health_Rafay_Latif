1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to www.concepthealthclinic.com and any pages hosted on it. It does not describe how we handle medical records created during your care — those are governed by our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, which is provided to patients directly and available on request at the clinic.

Concept Health Clinic is located at Gateway Central, 4616 US Highway 75, Suite 203 'A', Denison, Texas 75020.

2. This website is not a clinical channel

Please do not send medical information through this website. The general inquiry form, ordinary email and this chat are not secure channels for protected health information (PHI). Do not include diagnosis details, symptoms, medication lists, insurance member IDs or anything else you would consider private medical information.

For anything clinical, call us at (903) 600-2100 or use the secure patient portal. In an emergency, call 911.

3. What we collect

Information you choose to give us

If you submit the general inquiry form, we receive what you type into it: your name, email address, phone number, the inquiry type you select, and your message. We also record the time of submission.

Information collected automatically

Our web server and analytics tools record standard technical information: the page you requested, the date and time, your browser type, and an approximate region derived from your IP address. We use this to keep the site working and to understand which pages are useful.

What we do not collect

  • We do not create a profile of you across other websites.
  • We do not sell your information to anyone, for any purpose.
  • We do not use this website to make automated clinical decisions about you.

4. Website analytics and counters

This site displays a visitor counter and a call-click counter. Both are deliberately designed to be privacy-preserving:

  • The counters store a single integer — a total — and nothing about the individual visitor. No IP address, device identifier or browsing history is recorded against your visit.
  • Counting is done once per browser session so a single reader refreshing the page does not inflate the total.
  • Clicking a "call" button records that a call was initiated from this website. It does not record who you are or what you discussed.
  • If the counter service is unreachable — for example because you use a tracker blocker — the site falls back to a local count stored only in your own browser, and the page says so.

There is no advertising cookie, no cross-site tracker and no third-party remarketing pixel on this site. Because we do not use tracking cookies for advertising, no cookie consent banner is required.

6. How information is used

  • To reply to a general inquiry you send us.
  • To keep the website secure, working and fast.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which information is useful to visitors.
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations.

We do not use website information to make clinical decisions. We do not sell, rent or trade it.

7. Retention

General inquiry messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and for our ordinary business records, then deleted or archived according to our records policy. Aggregate counters hold a running total with no personal detail to delete. Server logs are retained on a short, rolling basis.

8. Your choices

  • Block the counters: a tracker blocker will stop them; the site still works and switches to a local count.
  • Skip the map: the map does not load unless you ask for it.
  • Ask us what we hold: email info@concepthealthclinic.com and we will tell you what website information, if any, is associated with you.
  • Ask for deletion: you may request deletion of a general inquiry you sent us, subject to record-keeping obligations.

Texas residents may have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. To exercise any of them, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

This site is served over HTTPS. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. No website transmission is completely secure, which is exactly why we ask you not to send medical information through these channels.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this site. Care for minors is arranged directly with the clinic by a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how this website handles information, we will update this page and revise the "last reviewed" date above.
